# Name and logo policy

The code of Locus is free software under the GNU Affero General Public License
(see [LICENSE](LICENSE) and [NOTICE](NOTICE)). The name "Locus", the Locus
logo and the other brand assets are not part of that grant: they identify the
official project and its service at [locus.now](https://locus.now).

## What you can do

- Run an unmodified copy of Locus for yourself, for a course, a workshop, a
  hackathon or inside your organization, and call it Locus.
- Say that your project is based on Locus or works with Locus, and link to the
  official repositories.
- Use the name in an article, a talk or a review about Locus.

## What you cannot do

- Present a modified version, or a service that runs one, as the official
  Locus, or as endorsed by the Locus project.
- Use the name or the logo as the brand of your own product or public service
  based on Locus. A fork that runs as a public service needs its own name and
  logo, and states that it is unofficial and based on Locus.
- Use a name or a logo that can be confused with ours.

## Questions

Open an issue in this repository to ask for permission or to report a misuse.
