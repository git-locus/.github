# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Locus to be a place where people feel safe sharing and discussing ideas. We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and kind. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intentions, and ask when something is unclear.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions, spam or trolling
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report problems to **reports@locus.now**. Reports are handled in confidence, and the privacy and safety of the person reporting comes first.

Maintainers may remove or edit comments, close or lock threads, and temporarily or permanently ban people whose behaviour breaks this Code of Conduct. They will explain the decision to the people involved.
